SURVIVOR POOL

Official Rules

Game Concept

Each player makes three preseason picks: two Winner Picks and one First Out Pick. Your score changes every week based on whether those contestants are still in the game.

Your Picks

  • Winner Picks (2): Any players may choose the same Winner Picks.
  • First Out Pick (1): Must be unique across the pool — no duplicates.

First Out Selection Order

🏝️ The First-Out Draft Ceremony

🏆 Last year’s pool winner picks first — champion’s privilege.

After the champion selects, all remaining First Out draft positions are determined by a random lottery.

Once a contestant is chosen as a First Out Pick, they are locked and cannot be chosen by anyone else.

This keeps First Out picks fair, unique, and appropriately brutal.

Weekly Scoring

+2 points per week for each Winner Pick that is still in the game (+4 total if both are still in).

−1 point per week while your First Out Pick is still in the game. Once they’re eliminated, the negative scoring stops.

Sole Survivor Bonus

If either of your Winner Picks becomes the Sole Survivor, you earn an additional +14 bonus points (awarded once at the end of the season).

Example

You pick A and B as winners, and C as First Out. If all three are still in after Week 1: you earn +2 (A) + +2 (B) − 1 (C) = +3.

Outwit. Outplay. Outlast. Then check the leaderboard.
