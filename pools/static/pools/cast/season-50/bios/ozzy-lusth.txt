A legendary Survivor competitor and entrepreneur from Guanajuato City, Mexico, he returns at age 43 as one of the most physically dominant and loyal players in the game’s history. Across Cook Islands, Micronesia, South Pacific, and Game Changers, he earned a reputation as a fierce challenge competitor and devoted alliance member, most notably as part of the iconic Aitu Four—an underdog alliance that overcame overwhelming odds to reshape the course of the game. His combination of athleticism, charisma, and loyalty made him both a valuable ally and a formidable opponent.

Since his last appearance, he has embraced an entirely new challenge: returning to his hometown to build Xolazul, a music venue, vinyl bar, and restaurant born from his passion for creativity and community. This ambitious undertaking required resilience, leadership, and an unwavering belief in his vision—qualities that mirror the demands of Survivor.

Known for his ability to form deep connections while competing with relentless intensity, he enters Survivor 50 determined to balance loyalty with decisive gameplay. Driven by his love for adventure and personal growth, he returns not just to compete, but to win—confident that his experience, adaptability, and competitive spirit still make him one of the most dangerous players in the game.
