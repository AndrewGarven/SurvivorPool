A real estate broker from Senatobia, Mississippi, now living in Germantown, Tennessee, he returns to Survivor at age 31 with unfinished business and a relentless drive to win. First appearing on Survivor 46, he quickly became one of the season’s most talked-about players, known for his bold personality, unpredictable gameplay, and willingness to take risks others wouldn’t. Whether admired or feared, he made an undeniable impact by playing authentically and keeping both allies and opponents on edge.

Since his first appearance, becoming a father has profoundly reshaped his perspective. Parenthood taught him patience, humility, and the importance of thinking ahead rather than trying to control every moment. His son now serves as his greatest inspiration, fueling his determination to succeed and prove that bold ambition and hard work can achieve anything.

Returning for Survivor 50, he plans to evolve his approach—starting with restraint and reliability before unleashing the strategic aggression that defines his game. Known for staying several steps ahead and influencing how others see the game, he thrives on controlling momentum without appearing to do so. With greater maturity, sharper awareness, and a deeply personal motivation, he returns ready to outmaneuver the competition and finally claim the title of Sole Survivor.
