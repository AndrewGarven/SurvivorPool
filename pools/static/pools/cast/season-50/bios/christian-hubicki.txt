A robotics professor from Tallahassee, Florida, originally from Baltimore, Maryland, he returns to Survivor at age 39 as one of the most intellectually creative and unconventional players the game has ever seen. First appearing on David vs. Goliath, he earned recognition for his analytical brilliance, humor, and innovative thinking—most famously applying a breadth-first search algorithm to locate a hidden immunity idol. His unique blend of scientific reasoning and social awareness established him as a formidable underdog and a memorable strategic mind.

Since his first appearance, he has founded and led his own robotics lab, guiding students and researchers in pushing the boundaries of technology and discovery. This experience strengthened his leadership, confidence, and ability to inspire others—skills he now brings back to the island. More recently, becoming a father has given his journey deeper meaning, fueling his determination to compete not only for himself, but for his son.

Now returning for Survivor 50, he approaches the game with a refined philosophy: Survivor is not just about votes, but about narrative. By shaping perception and controlling the story, he believes he can control the outcome. Armed with sharper strategy, deeper purpose, and unwavering resolve, he returns ready to finish what he started—and win.
