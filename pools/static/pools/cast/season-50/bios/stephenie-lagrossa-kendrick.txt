A three-time Survivor competitor from Glenolden, Pennsylvania, now living in Dunedin, Florida, she returns at age 45 with unfinished business and a fierce determination to prove she still belongs among the game’s elite. First appearing on Palau, followed immediately by Guatemala, and later Heroes vs. Villains, she established herself as a tough, competitive, and deeply loyal player. Her ability to navigate back-to-back seasons and advance all the way to the Final Tribal Council demonstrated her resilience, adaptability, and strength under pressure.

Since her last appearance, life has tested her in ways far beyond the island. As a mother of three, she has developed patience, perspective, and a deeper emotional resilience. The devastating loss of two brothers further strengthened her resolve, reinforcing her belief that perseverance and family are at the heart of everything she does. She returns not only for herself, but to honor their memory and inspire her children.

Known for her likability, loyalty, and competitive spirit, she plans to use her experience and maturity to build trust while avoiding early attention. Her strategy centers on leading through actions rather than words. Driven by purpose, resilience, and unfinished dreams, she returns ready to adapt, compete, and finally claim the title of Sole Survivor.
