A fire captain from Vacaville, California, now living in West Sacramento, he returns to Survivor at age 46 driven by purpose, resilience, and a promise he intends to fulfill. First appearing on Survivor 48, he earned widespread respect for his strength, loyalty, and emotional authenticity, winning four individual immunity challenges and demonstrating that leadership can come from compassion as much as competition. His vulnerability and unwavering commitment to his values made him a role model to his family and an inspiration to viewers.

Since his first appearance, he has faced profound personal challenges, including loss, healing, and reconciliation. Rebuilding his relationship with his ex-wife after years apart stands as a testament to his perseverance and belief in second chances. These experiences have deepened his emotional strength and reinforced his commitment to family, which remains the driving force behind everything he does.

Known for his loyalty and genuine nature, he plans to lean into authenticity rather than reinvent himself. His strategy centers on building real relationships and trusting that integrity can carry him far in the game. Motivated by the memory of his loved ones and the desire to honor his promise, he returns determined to push through any obstacle and prove he has what it takes to become the Sole Survivor.
