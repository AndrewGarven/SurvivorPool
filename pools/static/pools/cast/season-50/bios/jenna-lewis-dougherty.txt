A trailblazer from Survivor’s original era, the Franklin, New Hampshire native returns at age 47 to complete a journey that began with the very first season. Now a realtor and mother living in Woodland, California, she has built a life defined by resilience, independence, and determination. First appearing on Survivor: Borneo and later All-Stars, she proved herself to be far more than a symbolic early player—demonstrating sharp strategic instincts, bold gameplay, and an ability to influence the course of the game in ways that left a lasting impression.

In the years since, she has honed her skills as both a negotiator and relationship-builder, managing high-stakes real estate deals while raising her children. Her life experience has strengthened her confidence, adaptability, and ability to read people—traits that translate directly to Survivor’s social battlefield.

Returning for Survivor 50, she is driven by unfinished business and a desire to solidify her legacy. Known for her outspoken personality and fearless competitiveness, she plans to balance subtlety with decisive action, forming trust quickly while positioning herself strategically behind the scenes. Inspired by her children and her remarkable journey, she enters this milestone season ready to prove that her place in Survivor history was never a fluke—and that she still has what it takes to win.
