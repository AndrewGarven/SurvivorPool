A lawyer from Winnipeg, Manitoba, she returns to Survivor at age 34 as a thoughtful and fiercely strategic competitor determined to evolve her game. First appearing on Survivor 47, she quickly earned a reputation as a highly analytical and composed player, someone capable of separating emotion from strategy when necessary. Yet behind that calculated exterior is a deeply reflective individual who values connection, loyalty, and personal growth.

Since her first experience, she has gained a deeper appreciation for the human side of Survivor—recognizing that every player brings their own story, motivations, and vulnerabilities into the game. This perspective has strengthened her emotional awareness and reinforced the importance of balancing strategy with empathy. As a lawyer, she brings discipline, precision, and the ability to navigate complex interpersonal dynamics—skills that translate naturally to Survivor’s unpredictable environment.

Returning for the milestone 50th season, she is determined to step outside her comfort zone, allowing others to see both her strategic mind and her authentic self. Without former castmates to rely on, she plans to position herself as adaptable, useful, and quietly influential. Inspired by her past experience and driven by renewed confidence, she enters the game ready to play boldly, trust her instincts, and prove she has what it takes to become the Sole Survivor.
