A rancher and welder from Christoval, Texas, he returns to Survivor at age 51 as one of the most recognizable and respected competitors in the show’s history. First appearing on The Australian Outback, he quickly became a fan favorite for his rugged authenticity, quiet strength, and unwavering loyalty. Across All-Stars and Heroes vs. Villains, he solidified his reputation as a dependable ally and fierce competitor whose straightforward approach made him both trusted and underestimated.

Survivor altered the course of his life, and now, more than two decades later, he returns with a wealth of perspective gained through years of hard work, friendship, and personal growth. Sixteen years removed from his last appearance, he brings a calmer, more observant mindset—one shaped not by the need to dominate, but by the patience to listen, adapt, and strike when it matters most.

Known for his no-nonsense demeanor and predictable loyalty, he plans to use that perception to his advantage, embracing a more flexible and strategic style of gameplay. Grounded by gratitude and humility, he approaches this milestone season with quiet confidence. He knows that while skill and determination matter, Survivor also demands resilience, adaptability, and a bit of luck—and he’s ready to make the most of the opportunity.
