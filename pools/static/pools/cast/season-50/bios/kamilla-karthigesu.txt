A software engineer originally from Toronto, Ontario, now living in Foster City, California, she returns to Survivor at age 31 with sharpened confidence and a renewed competitive edge. First appearing on Survivor 48, she quickly established herself as a formidable and unpredictable player, earning two individual immunity wins on one of the most physically demanding seasons in recent memory. Her analytical mindset, adaptability, and willingness to make bold strategic moves made her a complex competitor—capable of appearing loyal one moment and dangerous the next.

Since her first season, watching the game unfold from the outside has strengthened her confidence and clarified her identity as a player. No longer weighed down by self-doubt, she returns with a stronger sense of self and a readiness to embrace the psychological and strategic warfare that defines Survivor. Inspired by her parents, whose resilience and selflessness shaped her outlook, she carries their strength with her into the game.

Known for her strategic flexibility and competitive drive, she plans to carefully manage how others perceive her while positioning herself to strike at the right moment. With nothing to prove and complete freedom in how she plays, she returns determined to outmaneuver, outlast, and ultimately claim the title of Sole Survivor.
