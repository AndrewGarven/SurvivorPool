An investment analyst from McKinney, Texas, now living in Laurel, Maryland, she returns to Survivor at age 30 with renewed confidence and a determination to rewrite her story. First appearing on Survivor 45, she quickly became known for her direct communication style, fierce independence, and willingness to stand her ground. While her first experience brought moments of growth and reflection, she left knowing she had more to prove—to herself and to the game.

Since then, getting married has strengthened her sense of self and social confidence, reinforcing her belief in the power of authentic relationships. She returns to Survivor 50 with a clearer perspective, embracing both her strengths and her lessons learned. Rather than changing who she is, she aims to evolve her strategy—remaining loyal and direct while becoming more flexible and open to unexpected alliances.

Unapologetically honest and unafraid of chaos, she understands that Survivor rewards players willing to take risks and seize critical moments. Inspired by her father’s lifelong encouragement to put herself out there and pursue opportunity without hesitation, she approaches this milestone season with bold intention. She’s not here to play it safe—she’s here to take control of her narrative and prove exactly what she’s capable of.
