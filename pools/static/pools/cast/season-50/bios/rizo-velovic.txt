A tech sales professional from Yonkers, New York, he returns to Survivor at age 26 as one of the youngest and most ambitious competitors on the milestone 50th season. First appearing on Survivor 49, he quickly made his mark as a fearless superfan with a bold strategic style. Known for his flashy gameplay, confident deception, and willingness to take risks, he demonstrated a deep understanding of Survivor’s mechanics, most notably holding a public immunity idol for eight tribal councils while maintaining influence and control within his alliance.

Fresh off his original season, he enters Survivor 50 with momentum, confidence, and a clearer understanding of how to navigate a cast filled with experienced legends. While his first game was defined by assertiveness and visibility, he now recognizes the importance of ego management, subtlety, and timing. His strategy centers on adapting his presence—blending in when necessary, building trust, and striking decisively when the opportunity presents itself.

Inspired by perseverance, personal growth, and the lifelong dream of competing on Survivor, he approaches this season with relentless determination. With unfinished business and a desire to cement his legacy, he returns ready to outlast the competition and prove he belongs among the greatest to ever play the game.
