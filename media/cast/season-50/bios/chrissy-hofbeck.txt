An actuary from Lebanon Township, New Jersey, now living in The Villages, Florida, she returns to Survivor at age 54 as one of the most formidable physical competitors in the show’s history. First appearing on Heroes vs. Healers vs. Hustlers, she stunned fans by tying the record for the most individual immunity wins by a woman in a single season, earning a reputation as an unexpected “challenge beast” whose determination and resilience defined her game.

Since her original appearance, her strength has been tested in far more profound ways. After learning she carried the BRCA gene mutation, she made the courageous decision to undergo preventative surgeries and later embarked on a 500-mile pilgrimage across Spain’s Camino de Santiago with her daughter. These experiences reinforced her belief in facing adversity with courage, discipline, and unwavering resolve.

A lifelong fan of Survivor, she approaches this milestone season with deep gratitude and clear focus. Her strategy centers on carefully managing her threat level while building trust and positioning herself strategically among competing alliances. Grounded by the support of her family and inspired by her own journey of perseverance, she returns with both the physical ability and mental fortitude to outlast the competition—and finally claim the title of Sole Survivor.
