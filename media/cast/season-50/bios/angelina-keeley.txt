A fan favorite from Survivor: David vs. Goliath, this San Diego–based entrepreneur returns to the game at age 35 with unfinished business and a renewed sense of purpose. Originally from Sparks, Nevada, she built her reputation as a bold competitor with big personality, fierce determination, and an uncanny ability to negotiate—famously securing rice for her tribe and earning the title of last woman standing after enduring all 39 days of one of Survivor’s most competitive seasons.

Since her first appearance, her most transformative life experience has been becoming a mother to her two daughters, Sophia and Gabrielle. Motherhood has strengthened her patience, resilience, and emotional depth—qualities she now brings back to the island. She describes this evolution as a “motherquake,” a powerful shift that has prepared her to play harder, smarter, and more intentionally than ever before.

Known for her confidence, charisma, and ability to forge deep personal connections, she plans to balance her bold gameplay with greater humility and vulnerability. Her strategy is simple: have fun, play hard, and win. Competing on Survivor’s milestone 50th season is both an honor and an opportunity—and this time, she’s coming for the crown.
