A four-time Survivor competitor and registered nurse from Jersey City, New Jersey, she returns at age 55 as one of the most iconic and socially dominant players in the game’s history. Across Panama, Micronesia, Heroes vs. Villains, and Game Changers, she built a legendary reputation for her emotional intelligence, strategic precision, and unmatched ability to form deep, authentic connections. Her gameplay consistently positioned her as a powerful force, earning her a place among Survivor’s most respected and formidable competitors.

Beyond the island, her greatest inspiration comes from her family—especially her grandchildren, who have given her a renewed sense of purpose and perspective. She is driven to show them that courage, determination, and belief in oneself can open doors at any stage of life. Her resilience is also shaped by her 103-year-old grandmother, whose lifelong optimism continues to inspire her every day.

Returning for Survivor’s milestone 50th season, she is focused on completing the journey she began two decades ago. Known for her socially lethal and strategic gameplay, she plans to once again rely on trust, loyalty, and instinct to navigate the game. With unfinished business and a legacy to fulfill, she believes the moment has finally arrived—it’s her time to become the Sole Survivor.
