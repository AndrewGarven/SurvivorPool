An artist and creative producer originally from Franklin Township, New Jersey, now based in Los Angeles, California, she returns to Survivor at age 34 with renewed confidence, independence, and a commitment to trusting her instincts. First appearing on Survivor 46, she quickly demonstrated her boldness and strategic awareness by locating a hidden immunity idol just two days into the game—an early signal of her willingness to take risks and play fearlessly.

Since her first season, she has embraced a major life transition, relocating across the country and rebuilding her life on her own terms. This experience strengthened her independence, resilience, and belief in her ability to navigate uncertainty—qualities essential for success in Survivor’s unpredictable environment.

Known for her honesty, creativity, and intuitive gameplay, she plans to approach Survivor 50 with a renewed mindset: focusing on each moment, adapting in real time, and trusting herself above all else. Rather than overthinking the future, her strategy centers on presence, awareness, and decisive action when opportunities arise.

Inspired by her sister’s strength and grounded in her own personal growth, she returns determined to play a game she can be proud of. With sharpened instincts and unfinished business, she is ready to embrace the challenge and prove she has what it takes to become the Sole Survivor.
