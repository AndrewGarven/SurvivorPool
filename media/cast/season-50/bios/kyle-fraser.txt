A defense attorney from Roanoke, Virginia, now living in Brooklyn, New York, he returns to Survivor at age 31 as the reigning champion of Survivor 48, determined to prove his first victory was no fluke. Known for his well-rounded gameplay, he combined emotional intelligence, strategic deception, and social adaptability to outmaneuver a competitive field and earn the title of Sole Survivor. His ability to form genuine connections while quietly advancing his own interests made him a dangerous and effective player.

Since his win, his life has transformed rapidly—he has moved cities, changed careers, officiated a wedding, and gotten married himself. These major life changes have strengthened his adaptability and emotional awareness, reinforcing the skills that helped him succeed in the game.

Returning for Survivor 50, he plans to lean into subtlety, presenting himself as approachable and non-threatening while carefully navigating the strategic landscape. He thrives in unpredictable environments and welcomes the twists and chaos that define modern Survivor. Inspired by creativity, personal growth, and the relationships that ground him, he approaches this milestone season with quiet confidence. If given the opportunity to build trust early, he believes his greatest strength—his ability to connect with anyone—will once again carry him to the end.
