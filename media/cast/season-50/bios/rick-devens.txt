A communications director from Blacksburg, Virginia, now living in Macon, Georgia, he returns to Survivor at age 41 as one of the most resilient and resourceful competitors in the game’s modern era. First appearing on Edge of Extinction, he built a reputation as a relentless fighter, winning four individual immunity challenges, conquering the grueling Edge of Extinction return challenge, and setting a Survivor record by playing four hidden immunity idols in a single season—two for himself and two to protect his allies. His fearless use of advantages and refusal to quit made him one of the most memorable underdogs of his season.

Since his first appearance, personal loss and life experience have reshaped his perspective, strengthening his appreciation for resilience, joy, and meaningful connection. These experiences have grounded him and reinforced his determination to compete with both intensity and gratitude.

Known for his scrappiness, loyalty, and ability to survive under pressure, he returns with a renewed focus on building deeper relationships while maintaining the bold creativity that defined his original run. Motivated by his family and driven by the belief that he thrives in adversity, he enters Survivor 50 ready to outwork, outthink, and outlast—confident that he can once again defy expectations and fight his way to the end.
