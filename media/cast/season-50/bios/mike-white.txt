A writer and director from Pasadena, California, now living in Hanalei, Hawaii, he returns to Survivor at age 54 as one of the most respected and quietly strategic players of the modern era. First appearing on David vs. Goliath, he demonstrated a masterful balance of social awareness and subtle influence, advancing to the Final Tribal Council and winning the critical fire-making challenge to secure his place among the game’s elite competitors. His calm demeanor and storytelling instincts allowed him to shape relationships and outcomes without drawing unnecessary attention.

In the years since, he has thrived in a high-pressure creative career, navigating complex personalities and demanding environments—experiences that mirror Survivor’s intricate social dynamics. These challenges have sharpened his leadership, emotional intelligence, and ability to guide narratives in unpredictable situations.

Returning for Survivor 50, he plans to lean into his greatest strength: storytelling. Understanding that Survivor is as much about perception as strategy, he aims to influence how players see both him and each other. Known as a likable yet stealthy strategist, he hopes to balance subtlety with decisive action. Inspired by his adventurous and resilient parents, he approaches this milestone season with humility, creativity, and a desire to help craft a Survivor story worthy of its legacy.
