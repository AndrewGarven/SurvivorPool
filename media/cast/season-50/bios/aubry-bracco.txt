A three-time Survivor competitor from Hampton Falls, New Hampshire, she returns at age 39 with a legacy defined by strategic boldness, resilience, and unfinished business. Across Kaoh Rong, Game Changers, and Edge of Extinction, she earned a reputation as a formidable thinker capable of navigating complex alliances and high-stakes decisions, most notably helping dismantle a powerful super idol and advancing deep into the game despite carrying a visible threat level.

Since her last appearance, life has profoundly reshaped her perspective. Supporting her father through a life-saving heart transplant—spending over 100 days alongside her family at Massachusetts General Hospital—taught her the true meaning of perseverance and hope. Shortly after, becoming a mother to her son, Odin, brought renewed joy, balance, and clarity. These experiences strengthened her emotional resilience and deepened her appreciation for human connection—qualities she believes are central to succeeding in Survivor.

Now also an entrepreneur who founded her own marketing business, she brings sharper instincts, decisiveness, and adaptability. Her strategy centers on building authentic one-on-one relationships and positioning herself as a trusted ally among strong competitors. Driven by curiosity, empathy, and fearless determination, she returns to Survivor not just as a strategist, but as someone ready to finish what she started—and claim the title of Sole Survivor.
