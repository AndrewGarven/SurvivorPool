A beach service owner from Gulf Shores, Alabama, he returns to Survivor at age 32 with a renewed perspective and a deeper appreciation for both the game and the life it shaped. First appearing on Survivor 42, he quickly earned a reputation as a hardworking provider—someone who excelled in challenges, contributed around camp, and gave everything he had until his final day in the game. His experience on the island proved transformative, instilling humility, gratitude, and a stronger sense of purpose.

Since his first appearance, he has continued to grow both personally and strategically, learning from some of Survivor’s greatest players, including close friend and mentor Boston Rob. These relationships, combined with his natural resilience and discipline, have helped him develop a more thoughtful and observant approach to the game.

Known for his physical strength and dependability, he plans to use that perception to his advantage while quietly strengthening his social and strategic positioning. Entering the milestone 50th season as a true free agent, he carries no preexisting alliances or expectations—giving him the freedom to adapt and forge new paths. Grounded by faith, family, and a relentless work ethic, he returns determined to play smarter, last longer, and seize the opportunity to become the Sole Survivor.
